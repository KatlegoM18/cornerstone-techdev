01 / THE IDEA

THE IDEA.

Surfers check the conditions before anything else. So on this site the conditions are not a widget in the corner. They are the design.

The look is built from three layers. The surf sets the sea and the headline: glassy, fun, pumping, blown out or flat. The time of day sets the sky, with the sun rising at the real sunrise and setting at the real sunset, and the moon and stars after dark. The weather goes on top: cloud greys the sky and hides the sun, and rain falls across the page.

The beach huts along the bottom are a nod to the real ones at Muizenberg. The school works out of hut number nine.

02 / THE BUILD

THE BUILD.

Live wave height, swell period, wind, water temperature and tide come from a free public marine API. A small scoring model knows that Muizenberg faces south, so it treats a north-wester as offshore (good) and the south-easter as onshore (bad).

The sea is drawn in code on every frame. Wave height sets how tall the lines stand, the period sets their spacing and speed, and the wind adds chop and whitewash. Colours and motion ease between states, so a change feels like weather rather than a page reload.

The sky is blended minute by minute, so a visitor who stays through sunset watches it happen. A three-day chart picks the best window to paddle out, and the lesson section changes its advice to match. Visitors can mix any surf, time and sky in a preview panel, and if the live feed is down the site says so and shows sample data.
