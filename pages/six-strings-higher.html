01 / THE IDEA

THE IDEA.

Most lesson websites open with a price list. This one opens with the instrument, because that is where every player starts.

As you scroll, the guitar comes apart piece by piece: the strings go slack and lift away, the tuners unscrew, the neck and headstock separate, and the soundboard lifts off the body. It is the whole journey from wood to sound, told without a paragraph of text.

Then the story turns. The pieces fly back together and land in the teacher's arms, so you meet the person and the instrument in the same moment.

02 / THE BUILD

THE BUILD.

A single guitar illustration was cut into 21 separate parts that line up to the pixel. The strings are drawn as live vector lines so they can bow, lift and pull tight again.

The teacher was illustrated in the same anime style, with his arms on their own layer, so the guitar genuinely slides in under his hands rather than fading in on top of them.

The whole sequence is driven by scroll and rebuilt for phones, from the lessons picker through to a working booking flow with a calendar and time slots.
