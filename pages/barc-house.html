01 / challenge

The challenge.

Barc House needed more of an online presence — one that could give potential residents a visual presentation of what the residence offers before they ever arrived for a viewing.

Instead of students having to travel from wherever they were staying simply to get a feel for the residence, its spaces, amenities and available accommodation, the experience needed to come to them through their screens.

Management also needed a better way to handle maintenance queries. Relying on simple WhatsApp messages made it easy for requests to become buried among other conversations or difficult to keep track of over time.

02 / solution

The solution.

We built an interactive website that gives students a clearer view of Barc House from their screens — including the residence, its amenities, available rooms and an applications section.

Behind the public-facing website, management has a secure portal where applications can be received, existing residents can be kept track of, and maintenance sheets can be stored and managed in one place.

Students can also be notified when their maintenance requests have been attended to and resolved, creating a clearer connection between residents and management.

// the build

One experience. Two sides.

A public-facing experience for prospective and existing residents, supported by a secure management portal built around the practical needs of running the residence.
