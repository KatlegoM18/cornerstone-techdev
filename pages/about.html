THE CORNERSTONE

I didn't start CornerStone because I had everything figured out.

I started it because I didn't.

I could feel the weight of the life I wanted sitting on my shoulders, while the reality of where I found myself felt further away than I wanted it to be. I knew I wanted to change that.

So I decided to build.

The name CornerStone comes from the biblical depiction of Christ as the cornerstone — the stone placed at the corner of a building's foundation, supporting and helping define the structure built upon it.

For me, CornerStone became more than a name. It became a reminder that every structure has to start somewhere.

This one started with the cornerstone.

THE PHILOSOPHY

Web design is digital art.

I've always had a passion for design. Whether it was drawing, writing, or simply letting my imagination run, my mind never really stopped creating.

Digital design gave that imagination somewhere to go — and a way to help other people express themselves too.

I believe corporate work shouldn't have to feel corporate. A website can communicate what a business does while also communicating how that business wants people to feel.

I don't want to simply make something people see. I want to make something they experience.
