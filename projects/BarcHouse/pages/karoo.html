Life at Karoo

More than just a place to sleep.

Karoo gives you your own private, furnished room — with the shared kitchens, lounges and outdoor spaces that make everyday student life a little easier.

Water, electricity and uncapped Wi-Fi are included in the rent, and the residence is protected by code access, panic buttons, electric fencing and gated parking.
