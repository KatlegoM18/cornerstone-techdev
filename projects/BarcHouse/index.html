Life at Barc House

Your ideal student home awaits.

Situated in Kempenville, Bellville, Barc House offers the perfect blend of comfort and convenience. Our single rooms are designed for male and female students, and each one is fully furnished to meet your needs.

With modern amenities and a welcoming community, we're committed to an exceptional student living experience.

Location

Close to CPUT and UWC's Bellville Medical Campus.

Barc House is just 4.6 km from the Cape Peninsula University of Technology (CPUT) and only 1 km from the Bellville Medical Campus of the University of the Western Cape (UWC), on the quieter side of Bellville, close to shops and public transport.

Before you apply

Important information.

  • Deposit: one and a half times the monthly rent, held as a security deposit until the end of the year.
  • Admin fee: R500, a once-off payment.
  • Rent: payable upfront for the following month.
  • Please note: Barc House is not NSFAS accredited.
