Welcome to Ijumba

A calm, well-made room in the heart of the northern suburbs.

Ijumba Signature is a small, modern hotel in Bellville. Every room has its own bathroom, air-conditioning and a hand-finished velvet headboard. Downstairs, there's a restaurant and bar, and someone at the front desk around the clock.
