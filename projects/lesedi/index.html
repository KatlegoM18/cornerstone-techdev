The estate

Three generations, one late hour.

The farm sits high on the western face of the Helderberg, where the False Bay breeze arrives every afternoon and the sun sets over the water instead of behind the mountain. That extra hour of light ripens the grapes slowly. The breeze keeps them fresh.

We pick by hand and ferment each block on its own, so each bottle tastes of one slope, one soil and one season.
