    Good to know

    Questions, answered.

    Something else on your mind? Mention it when you book and we'll get back to you before your first lesson.

    I've never played before. Is that okay?

    Completely. Foundations starts from how to hold the guitar and your first chord. Most beginners are playing simple songs within the first few weeks.

    Do I need my own guitar?

    It helps to have one to practise on between lessons. If you're not sure what to buy, ask in your first lesson and we'll point you in the right direction.

    How long is each lesson?

    Lessons are weekly and an hour long. Your monthly price covers every lesson in the month, plus a practice plan after each one.

    Can I switch paths later?

    Any time. Paths are a starting point, not a contract. If you'd be happier on a different string, we'll move you across.

    Acoustic or electric?

    Lessons are taught on acoustic guitar. Almost everything you learn carries straight over to electric if you switch later.

    What if I need to miss a lesson?

    Let us know at least 24 hours before and we'll move it to another time that week or the next.
